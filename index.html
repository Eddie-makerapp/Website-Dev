Our Story

Steeped in craft, rooted in ritual

Chá House was born from a deep reverence for the art of tea. We work directly with small farms in Japan's Uji region, Taiwan's Alishan mountains, Yunnan province, and the Darjeeling foothills — sourcing teas that tell a story. Every cup is brewed with intention, every pairing considered with care.
