Who We Are

Rooted in ritual

Chá House is a Cape Town institution built on a simple belief — that a great cup of tea is one of life's most generous gifts.

The Beginning

A love letter to the leaf

Chá House opened in 2012 in a quiet corner of Heritage Square, founded by tea sommelier Mei-Lin Jacobs after years of sourcing teas across Asia. What started as a tiny tea room with six tables has grown into Cape Town's most respected tea destination — without ever losing the intimacy of that first room.

Every cup we serve carries the story of the farmer who grew it, the season it was harvested, and the hands that prepared it. We don't just sell tea. We share a world.

What We Stand For

Our pillars

🌿

Direct Sourcing

We travel to farms in Japan, Taiwan, China and India to select our teas personally. No middlemen, no compromises.

🫖

Precision Brewing

Every tea is brewed at its correct temperature, for its correct time. We don't cut corners — not even on the water.

🍃

Seasonal Menu

Our pastry menu changes with the seasons. What's available today was made this morning. Nothing sits.

🏡

Community First

We host monthly tea education evenings, open to all. Tea culture should never be gated behind expertise.
